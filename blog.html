Justice for The Polly Family!

When most people get arrested and evicted it is their fault. It is rare that someone would be wrongfully arrested & evicted. To be clear my lease was terminated because of criminal activity and not because I was convicted of a crime. I plan on proving to a jury that I did not break the law & then I plan on sueing all parties involved. I will prove that this is a form of retaliation and that the EMT's lied in the police report. I encourage you to share our story and pray for us as my family has been devastated by these events. I feel violated on so many levels. I can't believe that the apartment complex is aloud to pick and choose who they can evict based off this criminal aligation law. The fact that they allow some people to live there that had criminal aligations and kick out others should be seriously investigated as selective enforcement. Many questions remain that will only come out in court one day. As of now the Judge in my case has given me 90 days to come up with the money needed for an attorney and my next court date is Feb-3-2027. I have lived in Oklahoma for around five years and have no criminal record here. These EMT's lying in a police report and the apartment complex lying about the damage done has made me a criminal. They punished me and my family on several levels. Weeks after the event I was arrested and spent the night in jail. After I bailed out on an OR Bond I no longer qualified to work at places like Instacart. Many jobs will not hire me now because they think I am a criminal with these charges. They made it hard for my family to get another place to live and we are living in a hotel now. This event will cost atleasg $2,500 for an attorney to be hired. I have reason to believe that the apartment complex is scarying my wittnesses. Cydi Amos did an audio testimony that Sharon said that the man who put on the door said it did not need a door at all just like Sharon says in her audio testimony. I feel like Sharon and Sandra are scared that the apartment complex will kick them out for telling the truth. In the end all these people will have to testify under oath in a court room and the truth will come out. Please help me and my family raise the funds needed to hire an attorney for criminal defense and an attorney to sue all these people after I win in court. Thank you for your time and support for my family in this time of chaos.
